Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Today View

One screen for your whole day — tasks, events, and habits, together. Overdue items surface automatically so nothing slips through.

Smart Tasks

Three task types: simple check-offs, checklists with subtasks, and ordered step-by-step flows. Each surfaces exactly what you need to act on now.

Scheduled Events

Add time-bound events to any day. They appear alongside your tasks in the today view and on your weekly or monthly schedule.

Week · Month · Someday

Switch between a 7-day week view, a sparse month view showing only days with items, and a Someday bucket for ideas without a deadline.

Recurring Items

Set any task or event to repeat on a custom interval — daily, weekly, every two months. Each occurrence is tracked independently.

Reschedule Overdue

A dedicated triage screen gathers every overdue task. Move them to today, tomorrow, or a custom date — one tap at a time.

Habit Tracking

Three habit modes — check-in, count (with custom units), and timed sessions. A 66-day streak bar shows your progress toward making it automatic.

Trash & Restore

Deleted tasks, events, and habits are soft-deleted and recoverable. Auto-purge after 7, 14, or 30 days — your call.

"All Clear" State

When every task, habit, and event is done for the day, WhenSpace celebrates you — and optionally surfaces your Someday ideas to keep momentum.

Habit engine

Build habits
that actually stick.

Research suggests it takes around 66 days to form a lasting habit. WhenSpace keeps a visual streak bar tracking your progress toward that milestone — not to stress you out, but to show how far you've already come.

  • ✓ Check-in habits — one tap marks the day done
  • ✓ Count habits — track glasses of water, pages read, reps done
  • ✓ Duration habits — hit start, stop when done, sessions accumulate
  • ✓ Archive on mastery — once you hit your streak target, the habit is marked mastered and filed away
