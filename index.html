Habits

Build habits
that actually stick.

Track time, count reps, or simply check in. Habits show their full history as a dot grid, while your streak grows in real time toward the point where the habit starts to hold.

  • ✓ Multiple habit types — time, count, or simple completion
  • ✓ Dot-grid history — see every completed window and every miss

Mastered

Mastered doesn't
mean finished.

When your streak hits the target, the habit graduates. WhenSpace checks in now and then — still doing it? Confirm and move on. If it slips, a short re-establishment series helps you get back on track.

  • ✓ Graduates automatically — habits leave the main list once the target is reached
  • ✓ Lightweight check-ins — confirm the habit still holds without daily noise
  • ✓ Easy restart path — re-establish the habit quickly if life gets in the way
