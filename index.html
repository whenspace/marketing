Tasks

Flexible enough for
how you actually think.

Some things need steps. Some need checklists. Some just need a day. Tasks in WhenSpace adapt to the work — break a project into ordered steps, track every item on a list, or park an idea with no date until you're ready for it.

  • ✓ Ordered steps — show the next action when a project needs sequence
  • ✓ Checklists — track every item when order does not matter
  • ✓ Someday tasks — park ideas with no date until you are ready

Habits

Build habits
that actually stick.

Track time, count reps, or simply check in. Habits show their full history as a dot grid — every window you completed, every one you missed. Your streak grows in real time, and your target tells you when the habit has truly taken hold.

  • ✓ Multiple habit types — time, count, or simple completion
  • ✓ Dot-grid history — see every completed window and every miss
  • ✓ Live streak target — know when the habit has truly taken hold

Notes

Capture anything.
Find it instantly.

Keep notes right alongside your tasks — no separate app needed. Meeting takeaways, quick thoughts, grocery lists, design decisions. One tap to capture, always there when you need them.

  • ✓ One-tap capture — write a note without switching context
  • ✓ Freeform text — anything from a quick thought to detailed meeting notes
  • ✓ Private by default — notes stay on your device, never sent to a server

Mastered

Mastered doesn't
mean finished.

When your streak hits the target, the habit graduates. WhenSpace checks in now and then — still doing it? Confirm and move on. The longer the habit holds, the less it needs your attention. Lapsed? A short re-establishment series gets you back on track.

  • ✓ Graduates automatically — habits leave the main list once the target is reached
  • ✓ Lightweight check-ins — confirm the habit still holds without daily noise
  • ✓ Easy restart path — re-establish the habit quickly if life gets in the way
