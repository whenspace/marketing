Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the WhenSpace marketing website (whenspace.app) uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

The WhenSpace app itself does not use cookies — it is a local-only Android application with no web component.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are commonly used to remember preferences, keep you logged in, or collect analytics data about how a site is used. Similar technologies (such as local storage) serve comparable purposes.

Cookies we use

Essential — consent record

We store your cookie preference in your browser's localStorage under the key ws_cookie_consent. This is not transmitted to any server. It simply remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies so we don't ask every visit.

Key Purpose Expires
ws_cookie_consent Stores your consent choice Until cleared

Analytics — Simple Analytics (optional)

With your consent, we use Simple Analytics to understand how visitors find and navigate the website. Simple Analytics is a privacy-first service — it does not use tracking cookies, does not build user profiles, and does not sell your data. It collects only aggregated, anonymised page-view data.

The Simple Analytics script is only loaded after you accept cookies. If you decline, no analytics script is ever loaded.

Service Purpose Privacy policy
Simple Analytics Aggregated page-view analytics simpleanalytics.com/privacy
