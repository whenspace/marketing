WhenSpace

A focused productivity app that unifies your tasks, events, and habits into one calm space — built for people who want less noise and more done.

What is WhenSpace?

WhenSpace is a personal productivity application for Android. It brings tasks, scheduled events, and habit tracking into a single, distraction-free view so you always know what to focus on today. Everything is stored locally on your device — no account required, no cloud, no subscription.

Our philosophy

Most productivity tools try to do too much. WhenSpace is deliberately small. We include only the features that directly help you plan, act, and build better routines — and nothing else. The goal is a calmer, clearer day, not another app to manage.
